# Intermediate Puzzles

## 50 Practice Questions with Answers and Explanations

1. There are 3 switches outside a room and 3 bulbs inside. You may go in only once. How do you determine which switch controls which bulb?

##### Answer :
-  **Switch 1, 2, and 3 are identified by the lit bulb and the warm bulb**

##### Explanation:

> Turn switch 1 on, wait a bit, then turn it off. Turn switch 2 on and enter the room. The bulb that is lit is controlled by switch 2, the warm bulb is controlled by switch 1, and the remaining bulb is controlled by switch 3.

2. You have a 3-liter bottle and a 5-liter bottle. How do you measure exactly 1 liter?

##### Answer :
-  **Use the 5-liter bottle to leave 1 liter behind**

##### Explanation:

> Fill the 5-liter bottle and pour it into the 3-liter bottle until the 3-liter bottle is full. This leaves 2 liters in the 5-liter bottle. Empty the 3-liter bottle, transfer the 2 liters, refill the 5-liter bottle, and pour into the 3-liter bottle until it is full. Exactly 1 liter remains in the 5-liter bottle.

3. There are 100 doors, all initially closed. 100 people walk by. Person 1 toggles every door, person 2 toggles every second door, and so on. Which doors remain open at the end?

##### Answer :
-  **The doors whose numbers are perfect squares**

##### Explanation:

> A door is toggled once for each divisor of its number, and doors with an odd number of divisors are left open. Perfect square numbers have an odd number of divisors.

4. A box has 8 red, 7 blue, and 5 green balls. What is the minimum number you must draw to guarantee at least one of each color?

##### Answer :
-  **16**

##### Explanation:

> In the worst case, you could draw all 8 red and all 7 blue before drawing a green. The next draw guarantees a green ball.

5. Two people start at the same point and walk in a circle. One walks 2 km/h faster than the other. After one hour, how far apart are they if the faster one is on the outer circle?

##### Answer :
-  **2 km apart**

##### Explanation:

> The faster walker covers 2 km more in one hour, so the distance between them is 2 km.

6. A man has 12 coins, one counterfeit. It is either heavier or lighter, but you do not know which. How many weighings to find it using a balance scale?

##### Answer :
-  **3 weighings**

##### Explanation:

> This is the standard optimal solution for 12 coins with one unknown odd coin.

7. A boat can carry 2 people at a time. There are 4 people: A, B, C, D. The boat must cross a river with a rule: A and B cannot be left together without supervision, and C and D cannot be left together without supervision. How do they cross?

##### Answer :
-  **C and D cross, D returns, A and B cross, B returns, C and D cross again**

##### Explanation:

> This sequence ensures the unsafe pairs are never left together without supervision.

8. A spider is at one corner of a cube and wants to reach the opposite corner. What is the shortest path?

##### Answer :
-  **Along three faces of the cube, length s√3**

##### Explanation:

> The shortest route is a path across three faces rather than through the cube. That path is shorter than any route that crosses more surfaces.

9. You are given 9 balls, one of which is heavier than the others. Using a balance scale twice, how do you find the heavy ball?

##### Answer :
-  **Weigh 3 versus 3, then 1 versus 1**

##### Explanation:

> Weigh 3 against 3. If one side is heavier, use that 3. Otherwise, use the remaining 3. Then weigh 1 against 1 to isolate the heavy ball.

10. A class has 30 students. 18 play football, 15 play cricket, and 8 play both. How many play at least one sport?

##### Answer :
-  **25 students**

##### Explanation:

> Use inclusion-exclusion: 18 + 15 - 8 = 25.

11. How many 1s are in the binary representation of 255?

##### Answer :
-  **8 ones**

##### Explanation:

> 255 in binary is 11111111, which has eight 1s.

12. You have 8 liters of water and two empty containers of 5 and 3 liters. How do you split 8 liters into 4 and 4?

##### Answer :
-  **Use the 5-liter and 3-liter containers in a transfer sequence**

##### Explanation:

> Fill the 5-liter jug, pour into the 3-liter jug until it is full, leaving 2 liters. Empty the 3-liter jug, move the 2 liters in, refill the 5-liter jug, and pour until the 3-liter jug is full. 4 liters remain in the 5-liter jug.

13. In a room of 23 people, what is the probability that two people share a birthday?

##### Answer :
-  **About 50.7%**

##### Explanation:

> This is the classic birthday paradox: the probability of a shared birthday becomes more than 50% with just 23 people.

14. A woman has three daughters. Each daughter has a brother. How many children does she have?

##### Answer :
-  **4 children**

##### Explanation:

> One son and three daughters satisfy the condition that each daughter has a brother.

15. You have a 10-minute egg timer and a 7-minute egg timer. How do you measure 9 minutes?

##### Answer :
-  **Start both timers and use the 7-minute timer as a 3-minute remainder**

##### Explanation:

> Start both timers at the same time. When the 7-minute timer ends, flip it. When the 10-minute timer ends, 3 minutes remain on the 7-minute timer, so the total elapsed time is 9 minutes.

16. If 3 men can dig a trench in 4 days, how long would 6 men take?

##### Answer :
-  **2 days**

##### Explanation:

> Doubling the workers halves the time if the amount of work stays constant.

17. A string is 2 meters long. It is cut into 3 pieces. What is the total length?

##### Answer :
-  **2 meters**

##### Explanation:

> The total length of a cut string remains the same regardless of how many pieces it is divided into.

18. A number is divisible by 3 and 5 but not by 2. What is the smallest such positive integer?

##### Answer :
-  **15**

##### Explanation:

> 15 is divisible by both 3 and 5 and is odd, so it is not divisible by 2.

19. Two roads cross at a right angle. One car is 5 km from the intersection and moves toward it at 50 km/h. Another is 12 km away and moves toward it at 30 km/h. When do they meet?

##### Answer :
-  **They meet after about 7.2 minutes**

##### Explanation:

> Their combined approach speed is 80 km/h, and the straight-line distance between them is 13 km at the start, so the time to meet is 13/80 hours, or about 7.2 minutes.

20. There are 3 people in a room: A, B, and C. A says, "B is lying." B says, "C is lying." C says, "A and B are both lying." Who is telling the truth?

##### Answer :
-  **C is telling the truth**

##### Explanation:

> If A and B are both lying, then C's statement is true, which makes the logic consistent.

21. You have 1000 liters of water. You need exactly 500 liters. You only have a 600-liter container and a 400-liter container. How do you do it?

##### Answer :
-  **Use the two containers to create a 200-liter remainder and then split it**

##### Explanation:

> Fill the 600-liter container, pour into the 400-liter container until it is full. This leaves 200 liters in the 600-liter container. Empty the 400-liter container, transfer the 200 liters, refill the 600-liter container, and pour into the 400-liter container until it is full. This is a standard container measurement technique.

22. You have 4 jars: one contains all red balls, one all blue balls, one all green balls, and one mixed. Labels are wrong. You may draw one ball from one jar. How do you label all jars correctly?

##### Answer :
-  **Draw from the jar labeled "mixed"**

##### Explanation:

> Since all labels are wrong, the mixed jar cannot really be mixed. One draw identifies the actual content of that jar, and the remaining labels follow by elimination.

23. A number is 4 times another. Their sum is 45. What are the numbers?

##### Answer :
-  **9 and 36**

##### Explanation:

> If one number is x, the other is 4x, so x + 4x = 45, giving x = 9 and the other number = 36.

24. A square is inscribed in a circle. If the circle radius is 5, what is the square's diagonal?

##### Answer :
-  **10√2**

##### Explanation:

> The square's diagonal equals the circle's diameter, which is 10. The diagonal of a square with side s is s√2, so the diagonal is 10√2.

25. In a hotel, there are 100 rooms. 50 rooms have windows, 60 have bathrooms, and 30 have both. How many rooms have either a window or a bathroom?

##### Answer :
-  **80 rooms**

##### Explanation:

> Use inclusion-exclusion: 50 + 60 - 30 = 80.

26. If 5 machines produce 5 items in 5 minutes, how many items do 10 machines produce in 10 minutes?

##### Answer :
-  **20 items**

##### Explanation:

> 5 machines produce 1 item per minute each, so 10 machines produce 2 items per minute. Over 10 minutes, that is 20.

27. The sum of two numbers is 100 and their product is 2500. What are the numbers?

##### Answer :
-  **50 and 50**

##### Explanation:

> Since 50 + 50 = 100 and 50 × 50 = 2500, both numbers are 50.

28. A circular track is 400 meters. A and B start together walking in opposite directions at 4 m/s and 6 m/s. When will they meet again at the starting point?

##### Answer :
-  **After 40 seconds**

##### Explanation:

> They move relative to each other at 10 m/s and cover the full track length of 400 meters, so 400 / 10 = 40 seconds.

29. A man has two ropes and each takes exactly 1 hour to burn completely, but they burn at inconsistent rates. How can he measure 45 minutes?

##### Answer :
-  **Light one rope at both ends and the other at one end**

##### Explanation:

> When the first rope burns out, 30 minutes have passed. Light the remaining end of the second rope. When it burns out, 15 more minutes have passed, totaling 45 minutes.

30. A dead-end street has 5 houses. Each house has a different color, and each owner owns a different pet. How can you determine the order with clues?

##### Answer :
-  **Use elimination with a clue table**

##### Explanation:

> Logic puzzles of this kind are solved by mapping each clue to a table and ruling out impossible combinations step by step.

31. In a grid of 8 × 8, how many squares are there in total?

##### Answer :
-  **204 squares**

##### Explanation:

> Count 1×1, 2×2, ..., 8×8 squares: 8² + 7² + ... + 1² = 204.

32. If you roll two dice and add the results, what is the probability the sum is 7?

##### Answer :
-  **1/6**

##### Explanation:

> There are 36 equally likely outcomes and 6 combinations produce a sum of 7.

33. You need to move a 3-liter and a 5-liter jar to get exactly 4 liters without measuring lines. What do you do?

##### Answer :
-  **Fill the 5-liter jug, pour into the 3-liter jug, then use the remainder**

##### Explanation:

> Fill the 5-liter jug, pour into the 3-liter jug until it is full, leaving 2 liters. Empty the 3-liter jug, transfer the 2 liters, refill the 5-liter jug, and pour until the 3-liter jug is full. 4 liters remain in the 5-liter jug.

34. A sequence follows 1, 4, 9, 16, 25. What is the next number?

##### Answer :
-  **36**

##### Explanation:

> These are perfect squares: 1², 2², 3², 4², 5², 6².

35. Suppose every second person in a line is removed, repeating until one remains. How do you identify the surviving position?

##### Answer :
-  **Use the Josephus problem formula or binary trick**

##### Explanation:

> This is the classic Josephus problem. The survivor can be found using recurrence or bitwise logic.

36. A number leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 2, 3, and 4. What is the smallest such number greater than 1?

##### Answer :
-  **13**

##### Explanation:

> Numbers leaving remainder 1 modulo 2, 3, and 4 are congruent to 1 mod 12, and the smallest such number greater than 1 is 13.

##### Answer :

39. Two numbers differ by 8 and multiply to 105. What are they?

##### Answer :

##### Answer :

##### Explanation:

42. What is the smallest number divisible by 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6?

##### Answer :
-  **60**

##### Explanation:

43. A train is 200 meters long and goes through a tunnel of 400 meters. If it moves at 10 m/s, how long is it inside the tunnel?

##### Answer :
-  **60 seconds**

##### Explanation:

> The train must cover 200 + 400 = 600 meters in total, and 600 / 10 = 60 seconds.

##### Answer :
-  **154**

##### Explanation:

##### Answer :
