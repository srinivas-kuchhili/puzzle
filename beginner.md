# Beginner Puzzles

## 50 Practice Questions with Answers and Explanations

1. A farmer has 17 sheep and all but 9 run away. How many sheep are left?
   Answer: 9 sheep are left.
   Explanation: The statement says “all but 9 run away,” which means 9 remain.

2. If it takes 5 machines 5 minutes to make 5 widgets, how long would it take 100 machines to make 100 widgets?
   Answer: 5 minutes.
   Explanation: Each machine makes 1 widget in 5 minutes, so 100 machines make 100 widgets in the same 5-minute interval.

3. You have a 3-liter jug and a 5-liter jug with no markings. How do you measure exactly 4 liters?
   Answer: Fill the 5-liter jug, pour it into the 3-liter jug, leaving 2 liters. Empty the 3-liter jug, pour the 2 liters into it, fill the 5-liter jug again, and pour until the 3-liter jug is full. 1 liter remains in the 5-liter jug.
   Explanation: This sequence leaves exactly 1 liter in the large jug. The classic measurement puzzle uses the two jug capacities to create target amounts.

4. A room has 2 light switches outside the room and 3 bulbs inside. You can only enter once. How do you identify which switch controls which bulb?
   Answer: Turn switch A on for a while, then off. Turn switch B on and enter the room. The lit bulb is B, the warm bulb is A, and the remaining bulb is C.
   Explanation: Temperature distinguishes the bulb that was on for a while from the one that was just switched on.

5. A train leaves City A for City B at 60 km/h. Another leaves City B for City A at 80 km/h. They meet halfway. Which train is farther from the station when they meet?
   Answer: Both are equally far from their respective stations.
   Explanation: They meet halfway between the stations, so each has traveled the same distance from its starting point.

6. If two cats catch two mice in two minutes, how many cats are needed to catch 100 mice in 100 minutes?
   Answer: 2 cats.
   Explanation: One cat catches 1 mouse every 2 minutes, so 2 cats catch 100 mice in 100 minutes.

7. What is the next number in the sequence: 2, 4, 8, 16, ?
   Answer: 32.
   Explanation: Each number is double the previous one.

8. A clock shows 3:15. What is the angle between the hour and minute hands?
   Answer: 7.5 degrees.
   Explanation: The minute hand points at 90°. The hour hand is 7.5° past 3 because it moves 0.5° per minute.

9. You have 3 boxes: one contains apples, one oranges, one mixed. All labels are wrong. You may pick one fruit from one box and then label all boxes correctly. How?
   Answer: Pick from the box labeled “mixed.” If it is an apple, that box is apples; then the remaining labels are deduced by elimination.
   Explanation: Since all labels are wrong, the mixed box cannot really be mixed. One pick reveals the true content of that box.

10. How many 3-letter words can be formed from the letters A, B, C if repetition is allowed?
    Answer: 27.
    Explanation: There are 3 choices for each of the 3 positions, so 3 × 3 × 3 = 27.

11. If a book costs $20 and you get a 10% discount, what is the final price?
    Answer: $18.
    Explanation: 10% of 20 is 2, so 20 − 2 = 18.

12. You have 10 coins, one is counterfeit and lighter. How many weighings to find it using a balance scale?
    Answer: 2 weighings.
    Explanation: Weigh 5 vs 5, then weigh 2 vs 2 from the lighter side to identify the fake coin.

13. A man buys a horse for $60, sells it for $70, buys it back for $80, and sells it for $90. How much profit did he make?
    Answer: $20 profit.
    Explanation: Profit is 10 on the first transaction and 10 on the second, totaling 20.

14. What is the next letter: O, T, T, F, F, S, S, ?
    Answer: N.
    Explanation: The sequence is the first letters of the numbers one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight.

15. In a family, each child has a brother and a sister. How many children are there?
    Answer: 3 children.
    Explanation: A family with 2 girls and 1 boy satisfies the condition that each child has a brother and a sister.

16. A rectangle has length 8 and width 3. What is its area?
    Answer: 24.
    Explanation: Area = length × width = 8 × 3 = 24.

17. How many sides does a decagon have?
    Answer: 10 sides.
    Explanation: “Deca” means ten.

18. If 1 + 1 = 10 in binary, what is 10 + 10 in binary?
    Answer: 10100.
    Explanation: 10 in binary is 2, so 2 + 2 = 4, and 4 in binary is 100. But with the statement conventions, 10 + 10 = 20, and 20 in binary is 10100.

19. You are in a dark room with 3 socks: 2 red and 1 blue. How many socks must you pull out to guarantee a matching pair?
    Answer: 2 socks.
    Explanation: In the worst case, the first sock could be red and the second could be blue, but a third is not needed if the objective is just a pair; to guarantee a pair, you need only 3 socks in many versions. However, with 2 colors, the standard answer is 3 socks in a general guarantee scenario.

20. What comes next: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ?
    Answer: 13.
    Explanation: This is the Fibonacci sequence: each term is the sum of the previous two.

21. A ladder is leaning against a wall. The top slips down 4 feet, the bottom slips out 3 feet. How much lower is the top?
    Answer: The classic interpretation gives a drop of 1 foot.
    Explanation: The ladder length stays constant, so the movement forms a 3-4-5 triangle relationship.

22. If a door is 2 meters wide and the frame is 2.5 meters wide, how much extra space is there?
    Answer: 0.5 meters.
    Explanation: 2.5 − 2 = 0.5.

23. 15 people are in a room. Everyone shakes hands once with everyone else. How many handshakes happen?
    Answer: 105 handshakes.
    Explanation: Use the formula n(n−1)/2 = 15 × 14 / 2 = 105.

24. A box contains 5 white balls and 5 black balls. How many must be drawn to guarantee one of each color?
    Answer: 11.
    Explanation: In the worst case, you could draw 10 balls of one color first, so the 11th guarantees a different color.

25. A person drives at 30 km/h for 2 hours. How far did they travel?
    Answer: 60 km.
    Explanation: Distance = speed × time = 30 × 2 = 60.

26. What is the largest 2-digit number?
    Answer: 99.
    Explanation: Two-digit numbers range from 10 to 99, so 99 is the largest.

27. If a cake is cut into 8 equal slices and 3 are eaten, how many slices remain?
    Answer: 5 slices.
    Explanation: 8 − 3 = 5.

28. A palindrome reads the same backward and forward. Give one example.
    Answer: “racecar” is a classic example.
    Explanation: Reading it backward yields the same word.

29. What is half of 100 plus 25?
    Answer: 75.
    Explanation: Half of 100 is 50, and 50 + 25 = 75.

30. A triangle has sides 3, 4, and 5. What kind of triangle is it?
    Answer: A right triangle.
    Explanation: 3² + 4² = 5², so it satisfies the Pythagorean theorem.

31. If a string of length 10 is cut into two equal parts, how long is each part?
    Answer: 5 units each.
    Explanation: 10 ÷ 2 = 5.

32. If today is Monday, what day is it 10 days later?
    Answer: Thursday.
    Explanation: 10 days = 7 days + 3 days, so the day moves forward three days from Monday to Thursday.

33. A man has 4 socks and 5 gloves. How many items must he select to guarantee a matching pair of one type?
    Answer: 3 items.
    Explanation: In the worst case, he could pick 2 socks and 1 glove, but the next item guarantees a pair by type.

34. What number is 3 more than 17?
    Answer: 20.
    Explanation: 17 + 3 = 20.

35. If a pack has 12 pencils and 4 are taken away, how many remain?
    Answer: 8 pencils.
    Explanation: 12 − 4 = 8.

36. A shopkeeper sells an item for $50 after a 20% discount. What was the original price?
    Answer: $62.50.
    Explanation: If the final price is 80% of the original, then original = 50 / 0.8 = 62.5.

37. How many edges does a cube have?
    Answer: 12 edges.
    Explanation: A cube has 12 edges total.

38. Three people can do a task in 3 days. How long will it take 1 person?
    Answer: 9 days.
    Explanation: One person does one-third the work per day, so it takes 3 times as long.

39. A coin is flipped twice. What is the probability of getting heads both times?
    Answer: 1/4.
    Explanation: The possible outcomes are HH, HT, TH, TT; only HH is a success.

40. What is the sum of the first 10 natural numbers?
    Answer: 55.
    Explanation: 1 + 2 + ... + 10 = 10 × 11 / 2 = 55.

41. If a room has 4 corners and one corner is removed, how many corners remain?
    Answer: 3 corners.
    Explanation: Removing one corner reduces the total by 1.

42. You have two jars: one with 10 red balls, one with 10 blue balls. You pick one from each. How many total balls are picked?
    Answer: 2 balls.
    Explanation: One ball from each jar means exactly two picks.

43. What is the smallest prime number?
    Answer: 2.
    Explanation: 2 is the smallest positive integer that has exactly two factors: 1 and itself.

44. If a bus leaves every 15 minutes and you arrive at a random time, how likely are you to wait less than 5 minutes?
    Answer: 1/3.
    Explanation: A wait under 5 minutes is 5 out of 15 minutes, which is one-third of the cycle.

45. A square has one side of 6. What is its perimeter?
    Answer: 24.
    Explanation: Perimeter = 4 × side = 4 × 6 = 24.

46. In a sequence, each number is doubled: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16. What is the 7th number?
    Answer: 64.
    Explanation: The sequence continues as 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, so the 7th term is 64.

47. What is 7 × 8?
    Answer: 56.
    Explanation: 7 groups of 8 = 56.

48. A man says, “I have three sons.” The first is named A, the second B. What is the third son’s name?
    Answer: C.
    Explanation: The pattern suggests the third son is named C.

49. What is the next number: 3, 6, 9, 12, ?
    Answer: 15.
    Explanation: The sequence adds 3 each time.

50. If a box weighs 2 kg and you add 3 kg, what is the total weight?
    Answer: 5 kg.
    Explanation: 2 + 3 = 5.
